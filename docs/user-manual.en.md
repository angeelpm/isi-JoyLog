# JoyLog — User Manual

JoyLog is your personal game diary — think Letterboxd, but for video games. Track what
you're playing, rate and review games, check live prices, and follow other players to
see what they're into.

**Access:** open **https://joylog.deushicest.org** in any modern browser (desktop or
mobile). No installation needed.

## 1. Getting started: account & sign-in

1. Go to **https://joylog.deushicest.org**.
2. Click **Sign Up** (top-right).
3. Enter a **username**, **email**, and **password**, then submit.
4. You're logged in automatically and sent to your dashboard.

To come back later, click **Login** and enter your email and password. Your session is
remembered on that device until you click the **logout** icon (top-right, next to your
username).

---

## 2. The dashboard

The dashboard (the home page, **Dashboard** in the top menu) is your command center:

- **Stats row** — Total Games, Playing, Completed, and Hours Played across your library.
- **AI recommendations** — game suggestions based on your taste (see section 11).
- **Recently Added** — the last games you added. Click any cover to open its details.
- **Your lists** — your collaborative lists, with a button to create a new one.

Use **Add Games** (top-right of the dashboard) at any time to jump to search.

---

## 3. Finding and adding games

1. Click **Search** in the top menu.
2. Make sure the toggle is set to **Juegos / Games**.
3. Type a title (or studio) in the search box. Optionally pick a **genre** from the
   dropdown to narrow results.
4. Press **Search**. Results load as game cards; scroll down to load more automatically.
5. Click a card to open the **game entry** window.
6. Set the status, rating and hours (see next section), then click **Add to Library**.

The game now appears in your **Library** and on your dashboard.

> A game can only be in your library once. If you've already added it, opening it again
> lets you edit your existing entry instead of creating a duplicate.

## 4. Tracking a game (status, rating, hours)

Opening any game (from search, the dashboard, or your library) shows the **game entry**
window. On the left you can set:

- **Status** — one of:
  - **Currently Playing**
  - **Completed**
  - **Backlog**
  - **Wishlist**
  - **Dropped**
- **Rating** — click the stars or type a number from **1 to 10**.
- **Hours Played** — total hours you've put in.

Click **Save Changes** (or **Add to Library** for a new game) to store it. To stop
tracking a game, click **Remove from library**.

## 5. Writing reviews & your diary

Each game keeps a list of **review logs** — dated notes about your playthroughs.

**To write a review:**

1. Open the game (it must already be in your library).
2. Scroll to **Log a new entry** on the right.
3. Write your thoughts. The current **rating** and **hours** are attached to the log.
4. Click **Add Log**.

Your reviews appear under **Review Logs**; reviews from other players show under
**Community Reviews**.

**Your diary:** click **Diary** in the top menu to see every review you've written,
across all games, in a single timeline sorted newest-first.

## 6. Checking game prices

Live prices come from IsThereAnyDeal:

- On **search result cards**, a compact price tag appears in the corner.
- Inside the **game entry** window, a price widget shows the current best deal.

Prices are informational and update automatically — no action needed.

## 7. Social: following players & the feed

**Find players:**

1. Go to **Search** and switch the toggle to **Jugadores / Players**.
2. Type a username and press **Search**.
3. Click a result to open that player's **public profile**.

**Follow a player:** on their public profile, click **Follow**. Click **Unfollow** to
stop. Their profile shows their stats, favorite games, public lists, and **games you have
in common**.

**The feed:** click **Feed** in the top menu to see recent activity (reviews and
completed games) from everyone you follow, newest first. Use **Load more** at the bottom
to see older activity. If you don't follow anyone yet, the feed points you to player
search.

## 8. Likes & comments on reviews

On any review written by **another** player (in a game's **Community Reviews**):

- Click the **heart** to like it; click again to unlike.
- Click the **speech bubble** to open the comment thread.
- Type a comment and press **Enter** or **Send**.
- You can delete **your own** comments with the trash icon.

You can read comment threads on your own reviews too, but you can't like your own reviews.

## 9. Collaborative lists

Lists let you group games by any theme ("Co-op night", "RPG backlog") and optionally
build them together with friends.

**Create a list:**

1. On the **dashboard**, find **Your lists** and click **Create list**.
2. Enter a **title** and optional **description**.
3. Tick **Public list** if you want others to see it (otherwise it stays private).
4. Click **Create**.

**Inside a list** (click it to open):

- **Add games** — search and add games to the list.
- **Remove games** — remove them with the trash icon.
- **Collaborators** — search for a player by username and add them. Collaborators can
  add and remove games too.
- The **owner** can remove collaborators and delete the list.

Public lists appear on your public profile so others can browse them.

---

## 10. Your profile

Click your **username** (top-right) to open **Profile**:

- **Bio** — write a short description and click **Save Profile**.
- **Favorite games** — search a game and add it; these show on your public profile.
  Remove one with the **✕** on its cover.
- **My lists** — your lists, with a lock icon (private) or globe icon (public).
- **View public profile** — see exactly what other players see.

---

## 11. AI recommendations

On the dashboard, the **AI recommendations** widget suggests games based on your top
genres, recently added titles, and how many games you've completed. Click a suggested
game to open it and add it to your library like any other game.

---

## 12. Troubleshooting

- **The page won't load** — check your internet connection and that the address is
  exactly `https://joylog.deushicest.org`.
- **"User not found" on a profile** — the username may be misspelled; try player search.
- **Can't add a game twice** — that's expected; each game lives in your library once.
  Open the existing entry to edit it.
- **Prices not showing** — the price service may be briefly unavailable; try again later.
- **Logged out unexpectedly** — your session expired; just log in again.

Still stuck? Contact the JoyLog team.
